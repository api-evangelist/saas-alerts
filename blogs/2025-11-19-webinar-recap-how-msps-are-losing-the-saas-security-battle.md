As remote work becomes the norm and BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) environments expand, SaaS applications like Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace have become prime targets for cybercriminals. At the same time, threat actors are changing their tactics. They aren’t breaking into networks or endpoints; they’re stealing identities.
