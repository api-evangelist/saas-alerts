Modern businesses run on SaaS applications. According to the State of SaaS 2025 report, a company uses approximately 106 different SaaS apps today.1 For MSPs, that means more responsibility than ever. Your clients rely on you to manage their SaaS tools, keep their users and data safe from evolving threats and everything in between.
